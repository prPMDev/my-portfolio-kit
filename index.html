Plainly

What this isn't.

Not a site builder

Bring your own (v0, Framer, Webflow, plain HTML, a designer), or let a coding tool like Claude Code or Cursor build it for you. The kit makes the words and keeps them current; you pick how the site gets built.

Not a template you clone

You don't pick a theme and pour yourself in. You bring your work; the words come out sounding like you.

Not magic that does it for you

It does the craft and the upkeep with you. You make the calls, and pick up how AI works on something you actually need.

Pick your path

the skills are linked, run them in order

You don't run every skill every time. Pick the route for what you're doing, left to right.

Whole portfolio, first time
setup find-my-voice content-strategist story-adapter portfolio-copywriter voice-guardian web-content-optimizer quality-check → then hand the content to your builder
Just one story
story-adapter voice-guardian quality-check → paste into your site
Just the words (hero, about)
portfolio-copywriter voice-guardian uses your saved voice
Keeping it current
refresh pulls in the right steps and loops you back
Optional, anytime
anonymizer a sensitivity pass for anything you're unsure about

Two rules of thumb: run setup and find-my-voice once, and always end a piece with voice-guardian, then quality-check, before it goes live.
